# Ansel's Pumpkin Patch: visual changes wanted

A brief for whoever redraws the art. The game is `games/pumpkin-patch/` (Phaser 3, `game.js`). Everything must match the existing chunky pixel-art style of the Ansel sprites: visible pixel blocks (about 5 source px per art pixel on the run frames), dark brown selective outlines, 2 to 3 tone shading, warm fall palette. No smooth vector art.

## 1. Ansel's outfit: denim overalls (highest priority)
- Reference: the owner's photo of Ansel at the pumpkin patch. Dark-wash denim overalls with shoulder straps, brass buttons and a bib pocket, over a white long-sleeve shirt (small dinosaur doodle print), grey and white sneakers.
- Replace the current "little dribbler" orange-badge tee and grey trousers in every pose: run frames 1 to 6, Up (arm raised), Down (crouch, reaching), damage (dizzy stars), and Lose if it's used.
- Keep his face, hair, proportions and head size identical. Leave out the baseball cap and any logos.
- Decide whether the outfit applies to Pumpkin Patch only or to the Uppah runner too. The runner (`dino.js`) uses the same shared sprites in `assets/`. For Pumpkin Patch only, put copies in `games/pumpkin-patch/art/ansel/` and point `preload()` at them.

## 2. Farmer look for harvesting
- When Ansel goes into the vine rows he puts on a straw hat and keeps it on. Today the hat is a separate prop (`art/straw_hat.png`, placed per pose by `HAT_POS` in `game.js`).
- Best: a hat variant of each pose, so it sits naturally on his hair. Otherwise, redraw the hat prop to fit cleanly in every pose.

## 3. New Ansel animations (from the photo's pick-up pose)
- **Pick up:** squat, both hands on the stem or hugging the pumpkin (like the photo), a tug (2 frames), then a strained heave for heavy pumpkins.
- **Carry:** a walk cycle with both arms up steadying a pumpkin on his head. Today he uses the normal run frames, so his arms swing while the pumpkin floats on his head. A heavier variant with a lean would be a bonus.
- **Water:** holding and tipping a watering can (today `art/watering_can.png` floats beside him).
- **Toss ("UPPAH!"):** today this reuses the Up pose and could stay.
- Keep the same frame canvas and feet position per pose, or update the `POSES` table (`headSrc`, `foot`, `h`) to match.

## 4. Scenery
- **Giraffe** walking behind the fence. Today it's a procedural 4-frame walk (`art/giraffe.png`, 4 frames of 224×352). Redraw it in the same style as the giraffe in the original Uppah zoo background (`assets/Background.png`), with a 4 to 6 frame walk and a "look around" frame. It must read at small size behind the fence rails.
- **Silo** beside the barn: concrete silo with an "ANSEL'S" sign band and a big grinning jack-o'-lantern on top. It's inspired by Goebbert's farm, but don't copy their name. Today the silo body is in `art/background.png` and the pumpkin is `art/silo_pumpkin.png` and `silo_pumpkin_wink.png` (normal and wink, 176×140).
- **Background** (`art/background.png`, 1920×2160) and the fence plus foreground layer (`art/foreground.png`, drawn in front of the giraffe): sky with clouds, sun, moon and stars higher up, hills, autumn tree line, red barn, fence, vine rows, dirt path. Upgrades: richer detail to match Ansel, clouds as separate layers that can drift, depth. Keep the same layout and the same ground line (the play path top is at y 2080; fence rails around y 1820 to 1860).
- **Gigi** (mom, red flannel): a proper cheer or clap pose for her cameo. Today she bounces on run frame 1.

## 5. Pumpkins and props
- Pumpkins (`art/pumpkin_*.png`): orange medium, large and small, deep-orange extra-large, white, green striped, warty green, gold, plus the rare golden one (`pumpkin_golden`), an unripe green one, and a sprout. Physics bodies use the sizes in `PUMPKIN_SPECS` (art px). Keep each pumpkin's body width and height, and its body centre, the same, or update `PUMPKIN_SPECS` to match.
- Other props: hay bale, leaf piles, falling leaves, speech bubble, wooden HUD panel, pointing hand, sparkle, dust, landing ring, water drop.

## 6. Coming from other workers, may need a polish pass later
- Gameplay agent: a big BOOM button (red plunger) and a windsock.
- Surprises agent: 12 to 20 small scenery surprises (barn animals, hayloft peeker, sun, birds, shooting star and similar).

## Constraints
- Transparent PNGs, nearest-neighbour friendly. World art is drawn at 4 game px per art pixel.
- Don't change the gameplay-relevant sizes without updating `game.js`.
- Today most art is generated by `make_art.py`. Hand-made replacements should either replace the generated PNGs with the generator updated not to overwrite them, or live under new names wired up in `preload()`.
